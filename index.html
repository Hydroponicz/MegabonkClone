JuggernautsA mini boss rolls in every 3 minutes. Wreck enough of them and the level boss comes for you.
WeatherEvery run rolls its own weather, shown as the forecast on the track card. Rain and snow cost grip, blizzards shove you around, thunderstorms drop marked lightning strikes, and dust storms send tornadoes across the track.
Exit portalBeat the level boss and a portal opens in a far corner. Drive in to end the run and bank it. Stay longer and Overdrive kicks in: tougher, faster cars every 40 seconds. Get wrecked before you escape and the run doesn't count.
TradersFrom about 2:00, a trader truck parks nearby every couple of minutes. Follow the light beam or the HUD arrow, drive into its ring and press F to buy items with bolts.
Trader's RollEvery level up can skip the bolt-on and roll a random item instead: Common, Rare or Epic, each with its own effect.
Account levelEvery run earns account XP. Higher levels open harder tracks.
UnlocksSome achievements add new weapons and upgrades to the level-up pool.
Loadout slotsA run carries 2 different weapons and 2 upgrades. Buy more slots in the Garage (Weapon Bay, Upgrade Rack).
