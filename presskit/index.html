Every level up lets you pick a new weapon or upgrade: swarm rockets, flamethrowers, hubcap saws, tesla arcs, rail lasers, attack drones and remote-control minions. No two runs build the same rig. Wreck two Juggernauts to draw out the track's boss, beat it, and race for the exit portal. Or stay for Overdrive and see how long you last.

Features

  • 5 tracks with their own bosses: Scrapyard, Outskirts, Neon District, Frostbite Flats and Inferno Raceway
  • 7 cars and 6 drivers, each with their own story, stats and skill tree
  • 14 weapons, 5 RC minions and more than a dozen upgrades
  • Changing weather: rain, thunderstorms, blizzards, dust storms and ash storms
  • Explosive barrels, lava, oil slicks, tesla pylons and props to smash through
  • Trader trucks, Chrome Bandits to chase down, and 30 achievements
  • Garage upgrades, paint jobs and vehicle skins between runs
  • Online leaderboards, and a short tutorial that gets new players driving in seconds
