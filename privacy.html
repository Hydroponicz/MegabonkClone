Privacy policy

Carnage Loop · Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information Carnage Loop (“the game”, “we”) collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is run by Zachary Smith. Contact: workwithhydro@protonmail.com.

The short version: Carnage Loop is made for players of all ages, including children under 13. There are no accounts, no analytics, no chat and nothing to type in. Your progress lives in your browser. A little game data goes to our server for the leaderboards, backups and the shop, and the ads are never personalised.

1. What stays on your device

Your save (progress, unlocks, settings, the name you choose, a random player ID and your recovery code) is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device unless one of the features below sends it. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it. (Reset progress in Settings starts you over but keeps a local backup copy in case you change your mind.)

2. What we store on our server

The game's server runs on Vercel and keeps data in an Upstash Redis database.

Vercel, our host, also keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and reliability. We don't use the player ID or recovery code to identify who you are, and we don't combine this data with other sources.

3. Advertising

On our website, ads are provided by Google (AdSense / H5 Games Ads). Because children play the game, we ask Google to show non-personalised ads only, for everyone: ads are chosen from things like the page and your general location, not from a profile of you. Google may still use cookies or similar technologies for frequency capping, measuring ads and preventing fraud; in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it asks for consent first through its consent message. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. More about how Google uses information for ads: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

On game portals such as CrazyGames or Poki, the portal serves the ads and its own privacy policy applies. In the mobile apps, ads are served by Google AdMob set up for child-directed, non-personalised ads rated for general audiences.

Watching an ad is always optional. Rewarded ads (a revive, double scrap, a sponsor chest) only start when you choose to watch them.

4. Purchases

Nitro Crystals can be bought with real money. Before any real-money purchase the game asks a grown-up to answer a question, so younger players can't buy anything by accident. Payments are handled by the payment provider (for example Stripe on our website, or Apple / Google in the mobile apps). We never see or store your card details. We only receive a confirmation of what was bought so we can add the crystals to your wallet, and we keep a record of that for accounting.

5. Children

Carnage Loop is for everyone, including children under 13. We designed it so we don't collect personal information from children: there's no account, no email, no chat and no free-text input; leaderboard names are generated by the game; ads are non-personalised; and the only identifiers we keep (a random player ID and recovery code, and IP addresses for about a minute) are used to run the game and protect it from abuse, which the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) allows without parental consent. Parents can ask us to see or delete their child's game data at any time using the contact above.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Get a new leaderboard name on the Leaderboards screen at any time.
  • Delete your data by emailing us with your recovery code (shown in Settings → Transfer save). We will remove your leaderboard entries, backups and wallet. Local data you can delete yourself, as described in section 1.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to use these rights. EU and UK residents can also complain to their data protection authority.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for personalised advertising.

7. Legal bases (EU/UK)

We process game data to provide the features you use (performance of a contract), keep the service safe from abuse (legitimate interests). We don't use personal data for personalised advertising.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
