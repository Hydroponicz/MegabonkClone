Privacy policy

Carnage Loop · Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information Carnage Loop (“the game”, “we”) collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is run by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO]. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version: there are no accounts and no analytics. Your progress lives in your browser. A small amount of game data goes to our server for the leaderboards, backups and the shop. Ads on our website are served by Google, who may use cookies.

1. What stays on your device

Your save (progress, unlocks, settings, the name you choose, a random player ID and your recovery code) is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device unless one of the features below sends it. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it. (Reset progress in Settings starts you over but keeps a local backup copy in case you change your mind.)

2. What we store on our server

The game's server runs on Vercel and keeps data in an Upstash Redis database.

FeatureWhat is storedHow long
LeaderboardsYour display name, a random player ID, and your best run on each track (time, wrecks, level, car, whether you escaped, date)Until you ask us to delete it
Cloud backupA copy of your save, filed under a one-way hash of your recovery code1 year after your last backup
Save transfer codesA copy of your save under a 6-letter codeDeleted when used, or after 15 minutes
Shop walletYour Nitro Crystal balance, owned skins and chest timers, filed under a hash of your recovery code2 years after last use
Abuse protectionYour IP address, used to limit how fast requests can be sentAbout one minute

Vercel, our host, also keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and reliability. We don't use the player ID or recovery code to identify who you are, and we don't combine this data with other sources.

3. Advertising

On our website, ads are provided by Google (AdSense / H5 Games Ads). Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, measure them, and, where you allow it, personalise them based on your visits to this and other sites. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, through Google's consent message. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

On game portals such as CrazyGames or Poki, the portal serves the ads and its own privacy policy applies. In the mobile apps, ads (if any) are served by the ad network named in the app's store listing, under its policy.

Watching an ad is always optional. Rewarded ads (a revive, double scrap, a sponsor chest) only start when you choose to watch them.

4. Purchases

Nitro Crystals can be bought with real money. Payments are handled by the payment provider (for example Stripe on our website, or Apple / Google in the mobile apps). We never see or store your card details. We only receive a confirmation of what was bought so we can add the crystals to your wallet, and we keep a record of that for accounting.

5. Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information (for example a real name used as a leaderboard name), contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Change your leaderboard name on the Leaderboards screen at any time.
  • Delete your data by emailing us with your recovery code (shown in Settings → Transfer save). We will remove your leaderboard entries, backups and wallet. Local data you can delete yourself, as described in section 1.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us to use these rights. EU and UK residents can also complain to their data protection authority.
  • We don't sell your personal information. Advertising partners may use cookies as described in section 3, and you can refuse personalised ads through the consent message or Google's ad settings.

7. Legal bases (EU/UK)

We process game data to provide the features you use (performance of a contract), keep the service safe from abuse (legitimate interests), and show personalised ads only with your consent.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
